I see a new freelancing model emerging among developers in the AI era: the part-time senior.

## The product is not always central

In a huge number of "tech" companies, the product is not really central. It only exists to support a business, whatever that business is. Fintech, edtech, healthtech: the software often plays a supporting role.

In these companies, there is often one person (the CEO, a partner, someone a bit technical) who can vibe-code the program or programs the business needs to run.

## The vibe-coding wall

But vibe-coding hits a wall fast:

- security
- scalability
- more and more bugs
- older features that break

## The fix: a senior, over time, part-time

A senior who knows the trade, who can come in for one or two weeks at the start to straighten everything out, put a [harness](/blog/ai-harness-guides-and-sensors/) around the AI agents, set guidelines, build a clean CI/CD pipeline... so the projects can keep being vibe-coded. Yes, I am a senior who encourages the habit, and I own it.

The results:

- an architecture that fits
- regular course corrections
- an eye kept on security
- a real senior over the long run

All of it for a fraction of a senior's monthly cost, since they work two to four days a month on the project.

## In practice

This is exactly the model I work with:

1. An intensive reset when needed: architecture, security, pipeline.
2. Regular follow-up for the life of the project: one day a week, two days a month... it is flexible.
3. The guarantee of someone who does not fall asleep on a technology.

There is also a version of this where there is no risky vibe-code in-house at all, and the senior simply comes in a few days a month to update and maintain what needs it.

This model answers a [broader shift in the developer market](/blog/the-developer-market-is-shifting/).
